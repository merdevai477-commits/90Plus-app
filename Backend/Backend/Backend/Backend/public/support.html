❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create an account?
Download the 90Plus app, tap "Sign Up," and choose either email registration or Apple Sign-In. Follow the prompts to complete your profile setup.
Why can't I upload videos?
Check these requirements: Video must be 5-60 seconds long, under 100MB, and in MP4/MOV format. Ensure you've granted camera and photo library permissions in your device settings.
How do I earn coins?
Earn coins by: Making accurate match predictions, completing daily quizzes, uploading popular videos, engaging with content, and spinning the daily wheel.
How do I block or report someone?
Go to the user's profile, tap the three dots menu, and select "Block User" or "Report User." You can also report individual posts by tapping the flag icon.
Can I delete my account?
Yes, go to Settings > Account > Delete Account. This action is permanent and will remove all your data, videos, and social connections.
Why aren't I receiving notifications?
Check: Device notification settings for 90Plus, app notification preferences in Settings, and ensure you have a stable internet connection.
How do predictions work?
Browse upcoming matches, select your predicted outcome, and optionally add a confidence level. Earn coins based on accuracy and confidence when matches conclude.
What languages are supported?
90Plus supports 8 languages: English, Arabic, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, and Turkish. Change language in Settings > Language.

🛠️ Technical Issues

Common Technical Problems

App Crashes or Freezes

  1. Force close the app completely
  2. Restart your device
  3. Update to the latest app version
  4. Clear app cache (Android) or reinstall (iOS)
  5. Contact support if issues persist

Video Upload Issues

  • Upload Fails: Check internet connection and file size (max 100MB)
  • Poor Quality: Record in good lighting with stable connection
  • Processing Stuck: Wait 5 minutes, then try uploading again
  • Format Error: Use MP4 or MOV format, avoid screen recordings

Login Problems

  • Forgot Password: Use "Forgot Password" link on login screen
  • Apple Sign-In Issues: Check Apple ID settings and two-factor authentication
  • Account Locked: Contact support for account recovery
  • Email Not Verified: Check spam folder for verification email

📱 Platform-Specific Help

iOS Users

  • App Store Issues: Update iOS to latest version
  • Apple Sign-In: Ensure two-factor authentication is enabled
  • Notifications: Check Settings > Notifications > 90Plus
  • Storage: Free up space if app is slow or crashing
  • Permissions: Settings > Privacy & Security > Camera/Photos

Android Users

  • Google Play Issues: Clear Google Play Store cache
  • Permissions: Settings > Apps > 90Plus > Permissions
  • Notifications: Settings > Apps > 90Plus > Notifications
  • Battery Optimization: Disable battery optimization for 90Plus
  • Storage: Clear app cache in Settings > Apps > 90Plus > Storage
