1. Information We Collect

We collect several types of information to provide, maintain, and improve our football social media platform:

1.1 Account Information

Authentication via Clerk: We use Clerk as our authentication provider to securely manage user accounts. When you create an account, we collect:

  • Email address (required for account creation)
  • Username and display name
  • Profile picture (optional)
  • Apple Sign-In credentials (if you choose Apple authentication)
  • Authentication tokens and session data
  • Language and region preferences
  • Account creation and last login timestamps

1.2 User-Generated Content & Social Features

Our platform enables rich social interactions. We collect and process:

  • Video Content: Short-form videos (reels) you upload, including metadata, duration, and thumbnails
  • Profile Customization: FIFA-style card designs, bio, favorite teams, and profile themes
  • Social Interactions: Comments, likes, reactions, shares, and follows
  • Match Predictions: Your predictions on football matches and associated confidence levels
  • Quiz Responses: Answers to daily quizzes across multiple categories (legends, transfers, current events)
  • Messages: Direct messages and interactions with other users
  • Reports & Moderation: Content reports, user reports, and moderation-related submissions
  • Blocking Data: Information about users you've blocked or who have blocked you

1.3 Device & Technical Information

Automatic Data Collection: We automatically collect technical information to ensure app functionality:

  • Device type, model, and operating system (iOS/Android)
  • App version and build number
  • IP address and approximate geographic location (country/region level)
  • Network connection type and carrier information
  • Device identifiers (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android)
  • Screen resolution and device capabilities
  • App usage patterns, navigation paths, and feature usage
  • Performance metrics, crash reports, and error logs
  • Push notification tokens for message delivery

1.4 Media Access Permissions

Camera & Media Permissions: With your explicit consent, we access:

  • Camera: To record and upload videos for reels and profile content
  • Photo Library: To select existing photos and videos from your device
  • Microphone: To record audio for video content
  • Storage: To temporarily store media during upload processing

Permission Control: You can revoke these permissions at any time through your device settings. Denying access will limit video upload features but won't prevent you from using other app functions like viewing content, making predictions, or taking quizzes.

1.5 Gamification & Engagement Data

To provide personalized experiences and rewards, we track:

  • Coins & Rewards: Coins earned through predictions, quizzes, and daily activities
  • Experience Points (XP): Points accumulated through app engagement
  • Level Progression: Your current level and progress toward next level
  • Achievements: Unlocked achievements and progress toward new ones
  • Daily Activities: Quiz participation, prediction streaks, and daily check-ins
  • Spin Wheel Data: Daily spin results and reward history
  • Leaderboards: Your ranking in various competitions and challenges

1.6 Football Data & Preferences

To personalize your football experience:

  • Favorite teams and leagues you follow
  • Match prediction history and accuracy statistics
  • Quiz performance across different categories
  • Content preferences and viewing history
  • Notification preferences for matches and events

5. Blocking & Privacy Controls

We provide comprehensive blocking and privacy controls to help you manage your social experience:

5.1 User Blocking System

When you block another user:

  • They cannot see your profile, posts, or videos
  • They cannot comment on or like your content
  • They cannot send you direct messages
  • They cannot follow you or see your activity
  • Their existing comments on your content are hidden
  • You will not see their content in your feed or search results

When someone blocks you:

  • You cannot access their profile or content
  • Your previous interactions with them are hidden
  • You cannot send them messages or follow them
  • The app will not notify you that you've been blocked

5.2 Privacy Settings & Controls

  • Profile Visibility: Control who can see your profile information
  • Content Privacy: Set default visibility for your posts and videos
  • Message Settings: Control who can send you direct messages
  • Notification Preferences: Customize what notifications you receive
  • Search Visibility: Control whether your profile appears in search results
  • Activity Status: Hide your online status and last seen information

5.3 Reporting & Safety Tools

  • Content Reporting: Report inappropriate posts, videos, or comments
  • User Reporting: Report users for harassment, spam, or policy violations
  • Harassment Protection: Additional protections for users experiencing harassment
  • Mute Options: Temporarily hide content from specific users without blocking

5.4 Data Associated with Blocking

Blocking Data Storage: We store information about blocking relationships to:

  • Enforce blocking restrictions across all app features
  • Prevent circumvention through new accounts
  • Provide safety analytics and improve protection features
  • Comply with legal requirements for harassment cases

Note: Blocking data is retained even if you unblock someone to maintain safety records and prevent abuse.

6. Video Upload & Media Processing

Video sharing is a core feature of 90Plus. Here's how we handle your video uploads:

6.1 Video Upload Process

Upload Pipeline:

  1. Recording/Selection: Use camera or select from photo library
  2. Local Processing: Basic compression and format optimization on device
  3. Secure Upload: Encrypted transmission to Supabase storage
  4. Server Processing: Additional compression, thumbnail generation, and quality optimization
  5. Content Scanning: Automated moderation and safety checks
  6. CDN Distribution: Global distribution for fast playback worldwide
  7. Publication: Video becomes available to other users

6.2 Video Specifications & Limits

  • Duration: 5 seconds to 60 seconds per video
  • File Size: Maximum 100MB per video
  • Resolution: Up to 1080p (1920x1080) supported
  • Formats: MP4, MOV, and other standard video formats
  • Audio: Stereo audio recording supported
  • Compression: Automatic optimization for mobile viewing

6.3 Video Metadata & Analytics

For each uploaded video, we collect and store:

  • Upload timestamp and device information
  • Video duration, resolution, and file size
  • Engagement metrics (views, likes, comments, shares)
  • Performance data (loading times, playback quality)
  • Geographic distribution of viewers (anonymized)
  • Thumbnail generation and preview data

6.4 Video Processing Technologies

Technical Infrastructure:

  • Expo AV: Video recording and playback functionality
  • Expo Image Picker: Media selection from device library
  • Expo Image Manipulator: Thumbnail generation and image processing
  • React Native Reanimated: Smooth video playback animations
  • Supabase Storage: Secure cloud storage with global CDN
  • Automatic Transcoding: Multiple quality versions for different devices

6.5 Video Privacy & Ownership

Your Video Rights:

  • Ownership: You retain ownership of your original video content
  • License: You grant us a license to display, distribute, and process your videos
  • Deletion Rights: You can delete your videos at any time
  • Download: You can request copies of your uploaded videos
  • Privacy Controls: Set visibility and sharing preferences for each video

6.6 Video Content Policies

  • Original Content: Upload only content you own or have permission to share
  • Copyright Respect: No copyrighted football footage, music, or other protected content
  • Community Guidelines: No violent, hateful, or inappropriate content
  • Privacy Respect: No videos of others without their consent
  • Authenticity: No misleading or deceptive content
