90Plus respects intellectual property rights and expects our users to do the same. This policy outlines our procedures for addressing copyright infringement claims in accordance with the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA) and applicable international copyright laws.

1. Copyright Infringement Policy

90Plus is committed to protecting the intellectual property rights of content creators, copyright holders, and rights owners. We take copyright infringement seriously and will respond promptly to valid DMCA notices.

1.1 Our Commitment

  • Promptly investigate all valid copyright infringement claims
  • Remove or disable access to infringing content when appropriate
  • Terminate accounts of repeat infringers
  • Maintain transparency in our copyright enforcement process

2. What Content is Protected

Copyright protection applies to original works of authorship, including but not limited to:

  • Videos: Football match footage, highlights, commentary, analysis
  • Music: Songs, soundtracks, audio recordings
  • Images: Photographs, graphics, logos, team crests
  • Text: Articles, commentary, written analysis
  • Broadcasts: Live match broadcasts, TV coverage

Important Note: 90Plus is a platform for user-generated content. Users are responsible for ensuring they have the right to upload and share content. Uploading copyrighted material without permission violates our Terms of Service and may result in account termination.

3. Filing a DMCA Copyright Infringement Notice

If you believe that content on 90Plus infringes your copyright, you may submit a DMCA takedown notice. To be valid and effective, your notice must include the following information:

3.1 Required Information

  1. Identification of the copyrighted work: Describe the copyrighted work you claim has been infringed. If multiple works are involved, provide a representative list.
  2. Identification of the infringing material: Provide the URL or specific location of the content you claim is infringing. Be as specific as possible to help us locate the content quickly.
  3. Your contact information:
    • Full legal name
    • Mailing address
    • Telephone number
    • Email address
  4. Good faith statement: Include the following statement: "I have a good faith belief that the use of the copyrighted material described above is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law."
  5. Accuracy statement: Include the following statement: "I swear, under penalty of perjury, that the information in this notification is accurate and that I am the copyright owner or am authorized to act on behalf of the copyright owner."
  6. Physical or electronic signature: Provide your physical or electronic signature.

3.2 How to Submit

DMCA Agent Contact Information

Email: dmca@90plus.app

Subject Line: "DMCA Takedown Notice"

Alternative Email: legal@90plus.app

Response Time: We will review and respond to valid DMCA notices within 48-72 hours.

Warning: Filing a false or fraudulent DMCA notice may result in legal liability. You may be liable for damages, including costs and attorney fees, if you materially misrepresent that content is infringing your copyright.

4. Our Response to DMCA Notices

4.1 Review Process

Upon receiving a valid DMCA notice, we will:

  1. Review the notice: Verify that it contains all required information
  2. Investigate the claim: Examine the allegedly infringing content
  3. Take action: Remove or disable access to the content if appropriate
  4. Notify the user: Inform the user who uploaded the content about the removal
  5. Document the action: Maintain records of all DMCA notices and actions taken

4.2 Timeline

  • Initial Review: Within 24 hours of receipt
  • Action Taken: Within 48-72 hours for valid notices
  • User Notification: Within 24 hours of content removal

5. Counter-Notification Process

If you believe that content you uploaded was removed in error or misidentification, you may file a counter-notification.

5.1 Required Information for Counter-Notification

  1. Your contact information:
    • Full legal name
    • Mailing address
    • Telephone number
    • Email address
  2. Identification of removed content: Describe the content that was removed and where it appeared before removal.
  3. Good faith statement: Include the following statement: "I swear, under penalty of perjury, that I have a good faith belief that the material was removed or disabled as a result of mistake or misidentification."
  4. Consent to jurisdiction: Include the following statement: "I consent to the jurisdiction of the Federal District Court for the judicial district in which my address is located, or if my address is outside of the United States, for any judicial district in which 90Plus may be found, and I will accept service of process from the person who provided the original DMCA notification or an agent of such person."
  5. Physical or electronic signature: Provide your physical or electronic signature.

5.2 How to Submit Counter-Notification

Email: dmca@90plus.app

Subject Line: "DMCA Counter-Notification"

5.3 Counter-Notification Process

  1. We will review your counter-notification for completeness
  2. We will forward your counter-notification to the original complainant
  3. The complainant has 10-14 business days to file a court action
  4. If no court action is filed, we may restore the content after 10-14 business days

6. Repeat Infringer Policy

90Plus has a zero-tolerance policy for repeat copyright infringers. We will terminate the accounts of users who repeatedly infringe the copyrights of others.

6.1 Strike System

  • First Strike: Warning and content removal
  • Second Strike: Temporary account suspension (7-30 days)
  • Third Strike: Permanent account termination

6.2 Serious Violations

For egregious or willful copyright infringement, we reserve the right to immediately terminate accounts without prior warning.

7. Fair Use and Exceptions

We recognize that copyright law includes certain exceptions and limitations, including fair use. However, determining whether a particular use qualifies as fair use is a complex legal analysis that depends on the specific facts of each case.

7.1 Fair Use Factors

Fair use may apply when content is used for purposes such as:

  • Commentary and criticism
  • News reporting
  • Educational purposes
  • Parody or satire
  • Transformative use

Note: Simply claiming "fair use" does not automatically protect you from copyright infringement. Fair use is determined on a case-by-case basis considering multiple factors. If you believe your use qualifies as fair use, you should consult with a legal professional.

8. Content Moderation

In addition to responding to DMCA notices, 90Plus proactively moderates content to prevent copyright infringement:

  • Automated Detection: We use technology to detect potential copyright violations
  • User Reports: Users can report suspected copyright infringement
  • Manual Review: Our moderation team reviews flagged content
  • Preventive Measures: We educate users about copyright and acceptable content

9. User Responsibilities

9.1 Before Uploading Content

Users must ensure they have the right to upload and share content. Ask yourself:

  • Did I create this content myself?
  • Do I have permission from the copyright owner?
  • Is this content in the public domain?
  • Does my use qualify as fair use? (Consult a lawyer if unsure)

9.2 Prohibited Content

Do not upload:

  • Official football match broadcasts or highlights
  • Copyrighted music or audio
  • TV show or movie clips
  • Professional photographs without permission
  • Content from other social media platforms without permission
