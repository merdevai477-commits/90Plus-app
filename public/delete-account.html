Delete Your Account

90Plus — Football Social App

You can delete your 90Plus account and associated personal data at any time. Choose one of the options below.

Option 1 — Delete in the app (recommended)

  1. Open the 90Plus app and sign in
  2. Go to your Profile tab
  3. Tap the Settings icon (gear)
  4. Scroll down and tap Delete Account
  5. Confirm deletion in the dialog
Open app settings

Option 2 — Email request

If you cannot access the app, send an email from the address linked to your account:

Email deletion request

What happens after deletion

  • Your account is deactivated immediately and you are signed out
  • Personal data is scheduled for permanent removal within 30 days
  • Some anonymized or legally required records may be retained as described in our Privacy Policy
Note: Deletion is permanent. You will lose access to predictions, reels, coins, and social connections tied to this account.
