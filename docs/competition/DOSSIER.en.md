# 90Plus — Competition dossier

**Product:** a mobile social platform for football fans.  
**Version:** 1.1.2  
**Platforms:** iOS and Android  
**Site:** [https://90plus.pro](https://90plus.pro)  
**Languages:** Arabic (first-launch default) and English

This dossier describes the product as it is built. It does not include usage or revenue figures.

---

## 1. Executive summary

90Plus puts a match day into one app.

Fans follow the score, lineup, and events as they happen. They predict the winner or the exact score and earn coins when they are right. They talk inside the match. They watch and post short clips. They take a daily quiz that is generated by a model and checked before it is published. They ask an assistant that fetches football data before it writes. Activity turns into XP, levels, and rank.

The product is shipping: an iOS and Android app, and an API on `90plus.pro`.

---

## 2. Problem

### Split attention

A fan currently reconstructs match day from tools that do not know each other:

- a scores app that does not know their friends
- a video network that does not know their club is playing
- a chat group where predictions are never settled
- a trivia app disconnected from this week’s fixtures

### Language

Many global football products translate the interface and leave the voice in English: push copy, quiz questions, and assistant answers. 90Plus is Arabic-first. The first launch is Arabic. English is a full second language.

### Hallucinated facts

A general chat model will often answer “how many goals in that final?” with a confident wrong number. In a sports product, a wrong number destroys trust faster than a slow screen.

### Live-data cost

Match-data providers cap daily requests. An app that calls the provider on every pull-to-refresh exhausts that cap on a busy slate and feels late to the user.

---

## 3. Solution

One loop, one identity:

```text
follow the match
    → predict, or enter a prize contest
    → discuss
    → watch or post a clip
    → take today’s quiz
    → climb the rank and invite a friend
    → come back for the next match
```

Coins, XP, follows, and notifications stay on the same account when the fan changes tabs.

The assistant and the daily quiz are not left to the model alone. The server owns entity selection, answer checks, and the tools that load a match or a player before any prose is written.

## 5. A match-day journey

1. Open the app and see today’s fixtures, including matches already in play.
2. Open a match: score, events, lineup, head-to-head, venue.
3. Before kickoff, submit a prediction. After the match it is settled once per user per match.
4. During the game, important events can push a notification. On iPhone they can surface a Live Activity.
5. After a goal, open reels or post a clip (5 to 60 seconds).
6. In the evening, play the daily quiz. Difficulty is mixed. Some questions are visual.
7. If a number is disputed, ask the assistant. It loads standings or a career, then answers.
8. XP and coins move. Rank updates. Share & Win can bring a friend into the weekly cycle.

## 6. Features

### 6.1 Live football

- Today’s fixtures and live matches, plus detail, lineup, standings, and top scorers.
- Player, team, and coach profiles, career paths, transfers, and stadium images.
- Two providers (API-Football and 365Scores) so coverage survives a gap in one of them.
- Redis and database snapshots so a screen open does not call the provider every time.
- WebSocket updates for the screen that is already open.
- Match-event push, and iOS Live Activities.

### 6.2 Reels

Community short video: like, comment, mention, hashtag, save, share, and view. Mux processes video. Cloudflare R2 stores images. The server enforces the limits: 5–60 seconds, 100 MB maximum, at most 10 hashtags.

### 6.3 Predictions and games

- One prediction per user per match. Submitting again updates that row.
- Coins are debited and credited inside a database transaction so the balance cannot go negative.
- **Prediction groups** with an invite code, members, and rounds.
- **King of Predictions,** a weekly accuracy contest.
- **Top Scorers Five.** The user picks five players from a league (Premier League, La Liga, Bundesliga, Serie A, Ligue 1). Points come from goals and assists in that gameweek. Picks lock before the gameweek starts.
- **Predict & Win.** A sponsor contest on one fixture. Entry can be free. The mode is winner or exact score. After full time the contest settles and winners are ranked up to the prize count. Prizes can be items or cash in Egyptian pounds. Statuses: draft, published, locked, settled, cancelled, rejected.
- **Share & Win.** A weekly cycle. Shares, referrals, score, and rank are computed on the server. A client cannot write its own standing.

### 6.4 Daily quiz

A daily pack in Arabic and English, with easy, medium, and hard questions. Types include knowledge and visual prompts (player, badge, stadium).

Pipeline:

1. The server selects the day’s entities from stored football data. The model does not pick the set.
2. Gemini writes the pack. OpenRouter is the fallback path.
3. Images are filled from the data providers.
4. A validator aligns the correct option with the entity and rejects inconsistent questions.
5. A static fallback pack exists so a failed generation does not mean an empty day.
6. Question metrics are stored so weak items can be reviewed.

A correct answer never reduces the score.

### 6.5 Football assistant

Arabic and English chat, streamed, with a voice quota so generation cannot be abused without a limit.

The assistant is a tool loop. It does not free-write the first token as the answer. Its tools include:

- football and player search
- today’s matches and live matches
- match detail and lineup
- standings and top scorers
- team info, squad, and scorers
- player career and a player’s match report
- head-to-head

Returned facts are grounded before the prose is streamed. Answers can deep-link to the player, team, or match screen inside the app.

Match chat is separate: public messages on one fixture, soft delete, reports, and block enforcement.

### 6.6 Progression

Coins, XP, levels, achievements, badges, a daily spin, and a public rank. Profiles present a football-game-style card: club, level, and activity.

### 6.7 Community and moderation

Follow, block, notifications, and reports. Strikes accumulate and are not silently reset. Banned words, warnings, and account bans exist. User content is soft-deleted so comments and reports keep their references.

**Age.** Social features sit behind an age gate. Underage users go through parental consent: request, waiting, then accept or decline.

### 6.8 Account and privacy

Authentication is Clerk, including Apple and Google. Terms acceptance is stored. Users can request a data export or account deletion. Consent and deletion actions are audited. This matches store and privacy expectations, including GDPR-style rights.

## 7. What to show the jury

### The assistant searches before it speaks

This is not a chat box painted onto a scores app. The model chooses a tool, the server runs it against live and stored data, then the model writes. The step count is capped so latency and cost stay bounded. If the model quota is tight, the answer is shortened instead of dropped.

### The quiz is generated under server control

The model does not freely choose the day’s subject and it does not publish itself. Entity selection, a confidence floor, and the correct-option check belong to the server. The model is one stage in a pipeline that can reject, retry, and fall back.

### Live data is cached on purpose

Hot reads come from the snapshot. The provider is called when the snapshot is missing or when a scheduled sync runs. A push is sent from a recorded match event, not from every client poll. The screen stays fast and the provider quota survives a crowded match day.

### Several games, one identity

A single prediction, a friends’ group, King of Predictions, Top Scorers Five, a sponsored contest, and a weekly referral all write to the same user and the same wallet. That is harder than one mini-game, and it is what makes tomorrow’s open natural.

## 8. Architecture

```text
Expo app (iOS / Android)
        │  HTTPS + WebSocket
        ▼
Express API (TypeScript)  ──  Clerk verifies identity
        │
        ├── PostgreSQL (Prisma)     accounts, cached football, reels, predictions, contests
        ├── Redis                   cache, rate limits, job queues
        ├── API-Football + 365Scores  fixtures and players
        ├── Mux + Cloudflare R2     video and images
        ├── Gemini / OpenRouter     quiz and assistant
        └── Expo Push + Live Activities
```

The marketing site at `90plus.pro` is a Vite build served by the same API process. The app is the product. The site is for introduction and share links.

### Why this split

- **The server owns the rules.** Balance, prediction integrity, contest settlement, and voice quota are not computed on the device.
- **Controllers stay thin.** A request is validated and passed to a service. Routes and background jobs share that service.
- **Multi-table writes use a transaction.** A coin debit and a prediction insert commit together.
- **User content is soft-deleted** so comments and reports do not dangle.

### Live match path

```text
provider
    → background sync
    → Redis snapshot + stored rows
    → WebSocket to the open screen
    → push queue when the event is worth a notification
    → the device
```

### Daily quiz path

```text
today’s entity slice
    → generation (Gemini, then fallback)
    → image enrichment
    → consistency and dataset checks
    → published pack, or the fallback pack
    → the user’s session and answers
```

### Assistant path

```text
user message
    → tool loop (capped steps)
    → tool runs against football data
    → facts are grounded
    → streamed prose + in-app links
```

## 10. Security and privacy

- Identity is Clerk. The current app path does not store passwords on this server.
- Write paths and account paths are rate-limited. Sensitive public routes have stricter limits.
- User, developer, and admin roles are checked on the server.
- Video and image uploads are checked for type, size, and duration before they go live.
- Free text passes banned-word checks. Reported content is filtered out of public feeds.
- Blocks are respected on public surfaces.
- Service secrets stay in environment variables. Only public client keys ship inside the Expo app.
- Sentry records crashes on the server and the app. Users never see stack traces.
- User-facing errors are plain language. Internal detail stays in logs.

---

## 11. Quality

- Pure game rules (for example Top Scorers Five points) are unit-tested without network access.
- Property tests cover product invariants: coin balance cannot go negative, a prediction cannot be duplicated, a correct quiz answer cannot reduce the score.
- Live-sync tests cover finished-match snapshots and a missing cache.
- Scripts check external-service connectivity and the notification path.
- Store binaries are produced with EAS (Expo Application Services).

## 12. Value

For the fan, the value is time and trust: match context, the clip, and the challenge in one place, and a football answer tied to a retrieved fact.

For a sponsor, the value is a contest that already runs: a fixture, a prediction deadline, a winner count, a prize, and settlement from the official result. The sponsor does not build a prediction system.

Return visits are designed: the quiz refreshes daily, the wheel is daily, the scorer gameweek locks and opens, and Share & Win is weekly. The product is not only a full-time notification.

This dossier does not forecast revenue. The commercial shape is visible in the product: sponsored contests, and daily activity that makes those contests worth running.

## 14. If a judge asks

**Why is this more than a skin on a scores API?**  
The score is the entry. The prediction, the group, the sponsor contest, the reel, the daily quiz, and the assistant all attach to the same user. A data provider does not build that.

**Why should we trust the assistant?**  
We do not promise perfect accuracy. We promise that an answer goes through a data tool before it is phrased, and that the prose is grounded on the facts that came back. That is more reliable than a general model writing a number from training data.

**What if a provider or the model fails?**  
Matches are read from the stored snapshot. The daily quiz has a fallback pack. The assistant shortens generation when quota is tight instead of failing without a trace.

**Is it safe for minors?**  
An age gate and parental consent sit in front of social features, with reports, strikes, and bans.

**Is this a concept or a build?**  
It is a build in production: app, API, database, queues, video, push, and AI paths.
